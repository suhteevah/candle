### Root cause hypothesis

The chunked CE forward computes loss-on-chunk inside a `while` loop,
each chunk's loss adding to a running `nll_sum` accumulator that
participates in autograd. The graph rooted at the final scaled_loss
holds backward pointers to every chunk's intermediate (logits, log
probs, gather, mask product). Under gradient checkpointing the per-
layer activations drop at layer boundaries, but the CHUNK loss
intermediates persist until backward — so we trade one large transient
that gets freed for N small transients that all stay alive.

At seq=128 with chunk=32 we have 4 chunks producing roughly:
  - logits: [1, 32, 151936] f32 = ~19 MB per chunk
  - log_softmax: same shape = ~19 MB per chunk
  - gather + mask product: ~5 MB per chunk
Total ~43 MB × 4 chunks = ~172 MB extra retained vs single-shot.
Baseline peak 7919 MB on 8192 MB card has ~270 MB driver overhead
margin — adding 172 MB pushes it OOM.
